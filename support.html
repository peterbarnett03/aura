My health data isn't showing up

Aura only reads Apple Health if you allowed it, and iOS never tells an app which categories you granted — so the app cannot warn you that a particular one is switched off. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Aura and confirm the categories you expect are on. If a category has no data in Health itself, nothing will appear here either.

Why is the weather chart empty?

It needs location access, and it needs days. Aura records one weather reading each day you open the app, and the comparison only appears once several days have accumulated in more than one pressure band — because a flare rate with nothing to compare it against is not a comparison. Give it a couple of weeks.

Why won't it show me a percentage?

Deliberately. Below about five flares, one more changes a percentage by tens of points, so the app shows plain counts instead and starts quoting proportions once there is a sample worth quoting. The same applies to the weather comparison and the trigger rankings.

My reminders aren't arriving

Medication reminders are local notifications, so they need notification permission — Settings › Notifications › Aura. Check the dose times on the medication itself too: they default to sensible hours, but if you take a dose at 7pm and the schedule still says 9am, the reminder will follow the schedule.

Can I get my data out?

Profile › Export Flare Data produces a PDF summary you can share or save. There is no cloud copy to request, because there is no cloud: everything is on the device.

How do I delete everything?

Profile › Delete All Data erases every record permanently and immediately. Deleting the app removes its database with it. Nothing is held on a server, so there is no further request to make and nothing for anyone to delete on your behalf.

Is Aura a medical device?

No. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and nothing it displays is medical advice. It is a record of what you logged and a tally of what recurred. Decisions about your care belong with your clinician.
